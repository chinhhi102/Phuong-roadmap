# 📚 Resources — Books, Courses, Tools & Certifications

> Curated, mostly free/affordable. You don't need all of them — start with the ⭐ picks.

---

## 📘 Books
- ⭐ **BABOK® Guide v3** (IIBA) — the reference standard. Skim, don't memorize.
- ⭐ **User Stories Applied** — Mike Cohn (the classic on user stories).
- **Business Analysis** — Debra Paul & James Cadle (great, readable intro).
- **Writing Effective Use Cases** — Alistair Cockburn.
- **Software Requirements** — Karl Wiegers & Joy Beatty (deep requirements reference).
- **The Business Analyst's Handbook** — Howard Podeswa.

## 🎓 Courses / Learning sites
- ⭐ **SQLBolt** (sqlbolt.com) — free, interactive SQL. Do this early.
- **Mode SQL Tutorial** (mode.com/sql-tutorial) — practical SQL for analysts.
- **Coursera** — "Business Analysis Fundamentals," IIBA-endorsed specializations.
- **Udemy** — search "Business Analysis" (look for high ratings, e.g., courses by BA mentors); also "BPMN," "Jira for BAs."
- **LinkedIn Learning** — "Become a Business Analyst" path.
- **IIBA.org** — official learning + certification info.

## 🛠️ Tools (all have free tiers)
- ⭐ **draw.io / diagrams.net** — BPMN, flowcharts, ERDs (free, no signup).
- ⭐ **Figma** — wireframes & prototypes.
- **Lucidchart** — diagrams (nice UX, limited free tier).
- **Jira** (Atlassian) — backlogs & sprints (free for small teams).
- **Confluence** — documentation.
- **Notion** — host your portfolio & notes.
- **Miro** — collaborative whiteboarding / workshops.
- **DB Fiddle / SQLite Online** — practice SQL in the browser.

## 📜 Certifications (in order of progression)
| Cert | Body | Best for |
|------|------|----------|
| ⭐ **ECBA** | IIBA | Entry-level — **start here** (little/no formal BA experience needed). |
| **CCBA** | IIBA | Mid-level (needs ~3,750 hrs BA work). |
| **CBAP** | IIBA | Senior (needs ~7,500 hrs). |
| **PMI-PBA** | PMI | BA within project management. |
| **PSPO / CSPO** | Scrum.org / Scrum Alliance | Agile / Product Owner skills. |

> **Your path:** Learn now → build portfolio → target **ECBA** while job-searching. It's designed for people entering the field.

## 🌐 Communities & ongoing learning
- **IIBA local/online chapters** and forums.
- **Reddit:** r/businessanalysis.
- **LinkedIn:** follow BA content creators; post your learning journey (great for visibility to recruiters).
- **BA blogs/podcasts:** Bridging the Gap (bridging-the-gap.com), Modern Analyst (modernanalyst.com).

## 🎯 Domain edge (use your MISA background!)
- Follow **accounting-software / ERP / fintech** BA content specifically.
- Learn the vendors' ecosystems (SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics) at a high level — your MISA experience translates.
