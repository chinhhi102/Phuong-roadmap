## 🎯 The goal

Become **job-ready for a junior/mid Business Analyst role** in **~10–14 weeks**, with:
- A solid grasp of BA frameworks (BABOK, Agile, requirements engineering)
- Hands-on skill in the core tools (BPMN, user stories, wireframes, SQL, Jira)
- A **portfolio project** proving you can do the work
- Interview readiness (technical + behavioral)

## 🧩 How our lessons work (the rules)

1. **One concept at a time** — no overwhelm.
2. Every lesson ends with a **1-question micro-challenge**.
3. I **wait** for your answer before moving on.
4. If a concept is hard, I **re-explain with a different analogy**, then try again.
5. We favor a **mix of theory + hands-on**, always tied to real examples (often from your ERP/accounting world).

---

## 📌 Daily habit checklist

- [ ] Read/learn today's concept (30–60 min)
- [ ] Do the hands-on exercise / micro-challenge
- [ ] Write 3 sentences summarizing what you learned (a "learning journal")
- [ ] Note 1 real-world example from your MISA experience that matches the concept
- [ ] Update your [Progress Tracker](progress-tracker.md)

> **Tip:** Teaching a concept back (even to an imaginary junior colleague) is the fastest way to lock it in.
