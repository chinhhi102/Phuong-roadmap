# 📕 Phase 4 — Interview & Job Readiness

> **Goal:** Convert your skills + portfolio into a **job offer**.
> **Est. time:** ~2 weeks · **Style:** practice, feedback, iteration.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- A **BA-tailored resume** and **LinkedIn** profile.
- Confident answers to **conceptual, scenario, and behavioral** questions.
- A **certification** plan.
- A clear **job-search strategy**.

---

## 📚 Modules

### Module 1 — Resume & LinkedIn for BAs
- Reframe your MISA role using BA language: *"Gathered and analyzed client requirements during ERP implementation,"* *"Mapped accounting processes,"* *"Facilitated stakeholder communication."*
- Quantify impact ("supported X clients," "reduced setup errors by Y%").
- Keywords ATS scanners look for: requirements, stakeholder, elicitation, user stories, BPMN, Agile, SQL, BRD.
- **Deliverable:** 1-page BA resume + optimized LinkedIn headline & About section.

### Module 2 — Presenting your portfolio
- A 3-minute "walk me through a project" story: problem → your approach → artifacts → outcome.
- **Deliverable:** rehearsed project pitch.

### Module 3 — Conceptual interview questions
Be ready to answer clearly:
- What does a Business Analyst do? How is it different from a Project Manager / Product Owner?
- Explain functional vs. non-functional requirements.
- What elicitation techniques do you know, and when do you use each?
- What is a user story? What makes a good one (INVEST)?
- How do you prioritize requirements (MoSCoW)?
- What is the difference between Agile and Waterfall?
- What is a Requirements Traceability Matrix?

### Module 4 — Scenario / case questions
- "A stakeholder demands a feature the team says is impossible in time. What do you do?"
- "Two departments give you conflicting requirements. How do you resolve it?"
- "How would you gather requirements for a mobile banking app?"
- **Practice:** structure answers as *Clarify → Analyze → Options → Recommend*.

### Module 5 — Behavioral interviews (STAR)
- **S**ituation, **T**ask, **A**ction, **R**esult.
- Prepare 5–6 stories (a conflict, a success, a failure/lesson, influencing without authority, handling change).
- **Tip:** Your MISA customer stories are gold here.

### Module 6 — Certifications roadmap
- **IIBA:** ECBA (entry) → CCBA (mid) → CBAP (senior).
- **PMI-PBA** (Professional in Business Analysis).
- **Agile:** PSPO / CSPO, or a Scrum foundation cert.
- **Recommendation for you:** target **ECBA** first — it's designed for people entering the field and signals commitment.

### Module 7 — Job-search strategy & "industry secrets"
- Apply to titles: Business Analyst, Junior BA, Systems Analyst, **ERP/Functional Analyst** (leverage MISA!), Product Analyst.
- Domain fit is a superpower — target **fintech, accounting software, ERP vendors** first.
- Network on LinkedIn; comment on BA content; join BA communities.
- Secret: hiring managers care more about *how you think* than perfect tool knowledge — show the "pause and ask why" reflex.

### Module 8 — Mock interview
- Full simulated interview (conceptual + scenario + behavioral), then feedback and a second round.
- **Deliverable:** you pass a mock end-to-end.
